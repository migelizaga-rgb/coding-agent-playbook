# Evidence Manifest

Use this index when a substantial rollout or handoff has evidence scattered across commands, CI, and operational records. Do not duplicate test output or create a manifest for routine changes.

| Claim | Evidence location | Result | Scope or timestamp |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acceptance criterion | Command, report, or artifact path | Pass, fail, or pending | Relevant revision and environment |

Include enough context to connect each result to the exact artifact. List failed or unavailable evidence explicitly. A manifest records evidence; it does not replace the evidence itself.
